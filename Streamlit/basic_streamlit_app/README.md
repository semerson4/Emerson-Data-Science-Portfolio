**World Happiness Report**

I made this app with the curiosity of seeing which countries are the happiest. I have organized the app into three parts, (which are all found in the visualization tab):

**Homepage**- This page does exactly what it says. It is the landing page for the app, contains the main titles and information, as well as providing background on the dataset and what its variables mean in the context of this app. 

**World Data Heat map**- This page is meant to help the user see the data on a world map and easily identify world happiness, as outlined by the variables on the homepage. 

**All Data Chart**- This page contains the original chart I made to start this app. It is a long and detailed chart that shows all variables and numbers contained in the datasets. This chart is there to help the user understand more about the data from the dataset, and contains more variables that may impact the to world happiness score, but are not looked into in this version of the app. 


**There are two interaction pieces to this app**: first being the visualizations tab on the side, allowing the user to navigate to different visualizations throughout the app and keeping the app as a whole organized and easy to read. The second visualization is related to the heat map where users interact through selecting what year (2015-2019) they want to look at world happiness. Users can also hover over the map to see what country name and happiness score they are looking at.
